One picture summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in world news: 33 stories
across 9 regions, built on the proven winners' template — red eyebrow,
giant serif date, the day in UTC, the day in numbers, three large hero
panels with original drawn illustrations, ten secondary cards, a world map
with numbered pins, a 20-story "also on Sunday" list, and a full-width
red DATE CHECK box listing the stories that were deliberately left out
because they happened on another day.

Top stories: COP31 collapses with no deal in Antalya; a Russian warship
sunk by sea drones at Benghazi; Democrats clinch 258 House seats, their
biggest majority since 1992. Every story here happened, or was first
reported, on Sunday 22 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) copies the pre-rendered
`entry.png` (1024×1024, 158,081 bytes) to `/output/entry.png`. The image
was rendered deterministically offline; the program itself needs no
network, no third-party libraries, and produces byte-identical output
on every run.
